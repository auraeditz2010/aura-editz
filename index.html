Design Tips

Small habits that make designs look professional.

01

Limit your fonts

Use one or two typefaces. Pair a bold heading font with a simple body font.

02

Use contrast well

Make sure text stands out from the background. Test readability on a phone screen.

03

Leave breathing room

White space makes a design feel premium. Do not fill every corner.

04

Build a color system

Pick 1 main color, 1 accent and 1 neutral, then stick to them across all work.

05

Align to a grid

Consistent margins and alignment make layouts feel organized and intentional.
